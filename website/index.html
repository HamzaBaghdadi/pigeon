Remote images blocked
  • Your mail stays on your computer
  • Tracking pixels blocked by default
  • Passwords kept in Windows Credential Manager
  • No ads, no analytics, no account with us

Everything you need, nothing watching you

A calm, quick inbox for all your accounts, built so that only you and your mail provider ever see your email.

Stored on your computer

Pigeon keeps a copy of your mail in a database on your own PC, so it opens instantly and works offline. Nothing goes through our servers.

Trackers stay out

Remote images, which senders use to see when you open their email, wait for your OK. Scripts in emails never run.

Secure sign-in

Sign in with Google happens on Google's own page, so Pigeon never sees your password. Other accounts use app passwords, kept in Windows Credential Manager.

New mail, instantly

Push delivery shows new messages the moment they arrive, with a notification. Pigeon can keep running quietly in the tray.

Fast search

Full-text search across all accounts, even offline. When you need older mail, search your whole mailbox on the server.

One inbox for everything

Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail and any IMAP account together, with all your folders and labels, keyboard shortcuts and undo.

Careful with attachments

Files that can run code are flagged and never opened directly. Saved attachments are marked as downloaded, so Windows scans them.

Make it yours

Three themes, each in light and dark: the clean default, soft pastel Blossom, and Gilded, a dark-fantasy look with a glowing golden tree.

Signed updates

Pigeon updates itself in the background and only installs updates signed with the project's key. Anyone can check how each release was built.

Pigeon and your Google account

When you choose Sign in with Google, Pigeon asks Google for the permissions on the right. They're used only to show, organize and send your email inside the app, on your computer.

  • Your Gmail data travels directly between your computer and Google. It never passes through a Pigeon server.
  • Your data is never sold, shared or used for ads, and no person reads your mail.
  • Your data is never used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Remove the account in Pigeon to delete its copy from your computer, or revoke access any time at Google account permissions.

Details are in the privacy policy.

  • Read, compose, send and manage your Gmail https://mail.google.com/ Lets Pigeon connect to Gmail over IMAP and SMTP to download, organize and send your mail. Gmail only lets email apps connect with this permission.
  • Your email address email Shown on the account in Pigeon and used as your Gmail sign-in name.
  • Your name openid profile Fills in the name people see on email you send. You can change it in Settings.
